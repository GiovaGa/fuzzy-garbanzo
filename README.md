# fuzzy-garbanzo

This repository contains notes and example notebooks related to facility location problems on graphs. A new notebook has been added at `notebooks/facility_location_graph.ipynb` demonstrating the uncapacitated facility location problem with brute-force and greedy methods.


## Bibliography

Our work in this repository is based on the following two works:

- Güvenç Şahin and Haldun Süral (2007). "A review of hierarchical facility location models." Computers & Operations Research, 34(8):2310–2331. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cor.2005.09.005. URL: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0305054805002959
  Summary: A comprehensive review of hierarchical facility location models (flow patterns, service availability, spatial configurations), applications, mixed integer programming models, and solution methods. The paper consolidates results from more than 70 studies dated 1986 or later.

- Reza Zanjirani Farahani, Masoud Hekmatfar, Behnam Fahimnia, and Narges Kazemzadeh (2014). "Survey: Hierarchical facility location problem: Models, classifications, techniques, and applications." Computers & Industrial Engineering, 68:104–117. DOI: 10.1016/j.cie.2013.12.005. URL: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cie.2013.12.005
  Summary: A comprehensive survey covering over 40 years of hierarchical facility location modeling. Models are classified by flow pattern, service availability, spatial configuration, objectives, capacity, time elements, and solution methods; the paper identifies gaps and suggests future directions.
